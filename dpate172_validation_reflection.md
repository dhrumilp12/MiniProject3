# Validation Reflection — dpate172

I reviewed David Long's (dlong37) ten projects across five fields, giving 50 comparisons. Before discussion, we agreed on 24 comparisons (48.0%); we resolved all 26 disagreements, reaching 100.0% agreement after discussion. RecentThemes had the most disagreements (8 of 10), followed by WhyRecovered (6 of 10). The [agreement statistics](dpate172_agreement_stats.csv) summarize my review of David's projects; they do not pool in his separate review of my projects. Our [validation log](dpate172_validation_log.csv) preserves the original judgments, while the [resolution table](dpate172_disagreement_resolution.csv) records the accepted outcomes.

The final ActivityPattern labels were six declining, three irregular, and one rising. Reduced activity sometimes preceded long gaps: geodesyNets fell from 133 commits in 2021 to six in 2022 before its 21-month gap, while TaxiSimulation's early development burst tapered off before its 81-month gap. These patterns suggest that shrinking activity can signal an approaching pause, but they do not establish why work stopped. Comparing the full monthly series and complete-year totals helped us distinguish long-term decline from occasional rebounds. Similarly, bursts in afex and Brainstorm did not by themselves establish predictable cycles; we adjudicated them as declining and rising, respectively.

Recovery often involved compatibility work or contributions from outside the original team. In afex, a reverse-dependency check identified incompatibility with the forthcoming ggplot2 release, providing a documented reason for the patch. geodesyNets resumed with Pietro Fanti's compatibility fixes and features, and TaxiSimulation returned with work adapting obsolete Julia syntax and dependencies. That return after 81 empty months shows why a long gap does not establish permanent project death. For other projects, the trigger remained uncertain. In OpenSAFELY, the long COVID extension followed the February restart; in SumOfSquares.jl, contributor documentation corrections preceded the maintainer's tutorial. We described those sequences without claiming that the later feature or earlier contribution caused recovery.

Definitions also changed several judgments. ANYbotics' README update counted as recovery after seven empty months, even though its discontinued-maintenance notice remained. Recovery therefore meant resumed repository activity, which could be limited to documentation. For Gaia Sky, the one-month longest gap still qualified for recovery analysis; the three-month threshold applied separately to NumberOfGapsInTimeline. Brainstorm had no empty month, so we retained N/A active project rather than labeling it as a failed recovery. These distinctions kept different questions about inactivity from being collapsed into one label.

We resolved four Currentstatus disagreements by using the written September 30, 2025 reference date and April 1-September 30 activity window. This made status reproducible, although Active remained a commit-based label rather than a measure of project health. RecentThemes required a different clarification: we used the latest ten commits in each agreed source snapshot, including snapshots extending beyond the status cutoff. Counting the allowed categories, classifying named merges by their substantive changes, and mapping standalone testing or bare merges to Other reduced differences caused by terminology and impression-based selection.

The categorical pre-discussion kappa values were 0.167 for HasRecovered, 0.342 for ActivityPattern, and 0.286 for Currentstatus. I retained N/A active project as a substantive third recovery category, so all ten projects remained in that calculation. Post-discussion kappa is 1.000 because both partners accepted the same final categorical labels; it measures consensus after adjudication rather than a fresh independent assessment. WhyRecovered and RecentThemes use NA for kappa as required by the README, and resolution time is NA because we did not track it. With only ten projects per field, the results are sensitive to individual decisions. The main improvement was making our definitions and evidence explicit while preserving uncertainty about motives the histories could not establish.
